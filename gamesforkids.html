Teacher & Family Resources

  • Pair younger learners with “reading buddies” to read instructions aloud and practice comprehension.
  • Use number and shape games as stations for fine-motor practice, counting, and color recognition.
  • Encourage families to play together at home and report one new vocabulary word or fact they learned.
  • Set timers and model self-regulation by reflecting on how breaks improve focus when returning to lessons.

Learning Benefits

Math & Shapes

Sorting, stacking, and number puzzles strengthen early numeracy and pattern spotting.

Literacy & Vocabulary

Story-driven adventures prompt learners to retell events, practice spelling, and build descriptive language.

Social-Emotional Skills

Co-op titles teach sharing, turn taking, and collaborative problem solving.

Kids Games FAQ

Are these games COPPA-friendly?

Yes. We avoid chat features, limit data collection, and review titles for age-appropriate content.

How can I share with families?

Use QR codes or LMS announcements linking directly to curated playlists so caregivers can supervise meaningful play.
